Getting started

A clear path from findings to verified repairs

  1. Choose a small scope. Start with disposable Confluence pages and review scan coverage.
  2. Inspect the evidence. Check the source page and destination. Authentication, timeouts and rate limits may require a manual check.
  3. Preview repairs. Review the exact before/after destination and affected occurrences.
  4. Apply deliberately. Only reviewed pages with current permissions and content checks should proceed.
  5. Verify and retain the audit. Review partial failures and keep the exported report with page version history.

When a result needs another check

For sign-in or access restrictions, verify the destination with the appropriate account. For rate limits and transient errors, retry later. A timeout is not proof of a broken link.

When a repair fails

Refresh the preview after content or permissions change. After an ambiguous write outcome, inspect the current page before trying again; avoid repeated blind writes.

Licensing and history

Commercial execution requires the app’s active Marketplace entitlement. Previously recorded evidence remains useful for review. Check the edition installed on your tenant and contact support if its license state is unexpected.
