Data security & privacy

How LinkOps handles data

LinkOps is a Forge app for Confluence Cloud. It processes Confluence page and link information needed to scan, classify, validate, compare, and safely repair links.

Data handling

Operational app data such as scan evidence, settings, snapshots, migration rules, and repair audit evidence is stored using Atlassian Forge storage in the current architecture. Ashbizcon does not operate a separate external SaaS database for LinkOps.

External destination validation

When external-link validation is enabled, LinkOps may send network requests from Forge to public HTTP(S) destinations referenced by customer Confluence content. This is necessary to determine whether destinations are healthy, redirecting, unavailable, rate-limited, forbidden, or otherwise require review.

Personal data

LinkOps is not designed to collect or store names, email addresses, profile photos, locations, job titles, or similar personal profile data in an Ashbizcon-operated external system. The app may process Confluence content and operational metadata required for its features.

Security controls

LinkOps uses reviewed Forge permissions, bounded background validation, server-side preflight for repairs, page-version checks, conflict protection, audit evidence, and post-fix verification. External requests are subject to validation safeguards intended to reduce unsafe destination handling.

Retention and support

Operational retention follows the behavior of the installed app and Atlassian Forge storage. For privacy or security questions, contact support.ashbizcon@mohitguruji.com.
