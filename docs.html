LinkOps for Confluence

LinkOps helps Confluence teams audit links, validate destinations, identify navigation gaps, preserve evidence, and run reviewed migration repairs.

Core workflow

  1. Scan Confluence content to inventory internal and external links.
  2. Validate internal targets and public external destinations.
  3. Review broken links, redirects, unresolved cases, orphan candidates, and exclusions.
  4. Preserve evidence by saving scan snapshots and comparing regressions over time.
  5. Repair reviewed migrations using exact URL, domain/path, or safe-regex rules.
  6. Verify repaired pages and retain audit evidence.

Safe Repair

Safe Repair uses server-side preflight, reviewed changes, page-version checks, conflict protection, audit evidence, and post-fix verification. LinkOps is designed to avoid blind overwrites when a page has changed after preview.

External validation

External checks can encounter rate limits, authentication walls, timeouts, or sites that block automated requests. LinkOps keeps those conditions separate from confirmed broken-link evidence where possible.

Automation and exclusions

Admins can configure scheduled evidence collection and ignore/exclusion rules for known destinations that should not be probed.

CSV workflows

LinkOps supports CSV export for link evidence and migration-rule workflows, including validated rule import/export where available in the installed app version.
