Support

Customer support

For LinkOps product help, operational incidents, or security reports, email support.ashbizcon@mohitguruji.com.

What to include

  • LinkOps version, if visible.
  • Your Confluence Cloud site domain if appropriate for support.
  • Approximate time of the problem.
  • The feature involved: scan, validation, history, migration preview, repair, verification, exclusions, or scheduling.
  • Visible error text and any LinkOps audit/run id.
  • Whether the issue can be reproduced on disposable test content.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, authentication cookies, customer secrets, or unrelated confidential page content.

Repair incidents

Retain the LinkOps audit CSV and Confluence page version history. Do not repeatedly apply the same repair after an ambiguous failure. LinkOps intentionally avoids blind retries for content writes.

External-link validation

Public destinations may block automated requests, require authentication, rate-limit, or behave differently for HEAD and GET requests. LinkOps distinguishes these conditions from confirmed broken-link evidence where possible.
